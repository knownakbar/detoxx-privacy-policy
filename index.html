Detoxx Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Detoxx ("Detoxx", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Detoxx Android application.

Developer: Md Jalaluddin
App: Detoxx
Contact: detoxxsupport@gmail.com

1. Privacy at a Glance

Detoxx is designed as a local-first digital wellness and self-control application. The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal profile information to use its core functionality.

Detoxx may use Android system capabilities such as Usage Access, Accessibility Services, and VPN-based filtering where enabled by the user. These capabilities are used to provide app usage monitoring, app restriction, digital wellness, and content-filtering features.

2. Information Stored by Detoxx

Detoxx may store information locally on your Android device in order to provide its features.

This may include:

  • Detox session start and end times
  • Selected apps for restriction
  • Detox session status
  • Detox progress and completion information
  • Emergency Break state and extension information
  • App preferences and settings
  • Learning/article progress
  • Permission and capability status needed for the App to operate

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is not intended to be used to create a personal account or advertising profile by Detoxx.

3. No Mandatory Account

Detoxx does not require a user account, login, or password for its core functionality.

We do not require users to submit their name, phone number, address, or other personal profile information merely to use the core Detoxx functionality.

4. Android Usage Access

Detoxx may request Android Usage Access permission to understand which applications are being used and for how long.

This capability is used for features such as screen-time statistics, usage monitoring, progress information, and digital-wellness controls.

Detoxx does not use Usage Access to collect unrelated personal information.

5. Accessibility Service

Detoxx may use an Android Accessibility Service when the user explicitly enables it.

The Accessibility capability may be used to support legitimate digital wellness functionality such as detecting access to selected applications, enforcing user-selected restrictions, and supporting content or distraction controls.

Detoxx does not use the Accessibility Service to read passwords, banking credentials, or other unrelated sensitive information.

Users can disable the Accessibility Service through Android system settings. Some Detoxx features may stop working when the service is disabled.

6. VPN and Content Filtering

Where enabled and supported by the Android device, Detoxx may use Android VPN functionality for content-filtering and digital-wellness purposes.

VPN functionality may be used to help identify or restrict network destinations according to the user's selected filtering configuration.

Detoxx does not sell browsing history or intentionally create a personal browsing profile from VPN activity.

Android allows only one active VPN connection in many configurations. Therefore, another VPN, Private DNS configuration, device restriction, or manufacturer-specific behavior may prevent Detoxx filtering from operating correctly.

7. App Restrictions and Digital Wellness

When a user starts a Detox session, the App may use supported Android capabilities to restrict selected applications according to the user's configuration.

Detoxx does not guarantee that every Android device or every application can be restricted in exactly the same way. Android version, device manufacturer, system settings, permissions, battery management, and other system limitations may affect functionality.

8. Emergency Break

Detoxx provides an Emergency Break feature for active Detox sessions.

The current Emergency Break configuration provides:

  • One free Emergency Break of 30 minutes
  • Additional extensions of 30 minutes each
  • Each additional 30-minute extension costs ₹99
  • Additional extensions may be purchased as supported by the App

An Emergency Break does not change the original Detox session's authoritative start or end time. It temporarily provides the permitted break period and the protection is restored according to the App's session rules.

Any paid extension requires an explicit purchase action by the user. Detoxx does not intentionally perform hidden or automatic charges.

9. Google Play Billing

Detoxx may use Google Play Billing for paid digital features, including supported Emergency Break extensions and other paid features offered through Google Play.

Payment card, bank, UPI, or other payment credentials are processed by Google Play and are not directly collected or stored by Detoxx.

Purchase records required to verify purchases and provide purchased features may be processed through Google Play's billing systems.

Google Play's own terms and privacy practices also apply to transactions made through Google Play.

10. Advertising

Detoxx may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may process information according to their own policies for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and consent management.

Where required, Detoxx may display Google's consent management experience so users can make applicable privacy and advertising choices.

Ads are not intended to be shown during sensitive user flows such as an active Detox protection screen, Emergency Break purchase flow, or other flows where displaying an advertisement would interfere with the core protection experience.

11. Third-Party Services

Detoxx may rely on third-party services to provide specific functionality. These services may include:

  • Google Play services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Android operating-system services

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

12. Data Sharing

Detoxx does not sell users' personal information.

Core Detox session information is designed to remain local to the user's device unless a particular feature or third-party service requires information to be processed by that service.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Google when required to provide advertising, consent management, application distribution, or payment functionality.

13. Data Security

Detoxx uses local storage and Android platform security mechanisms appropriate to the application's functionality.

However, no application or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their Android device secured with an appropriate screen lock and current security updates.

14. Data Retention

Detoxx retains local application data on the user's device for as long as it is required to provide the application's functionality, unless the user removes the data or uninstalls the application, subject to Android system behavior and active-session protection requirements.

Some information associated with Google Play purchases or advertising may be retained by the respective third-party service according to its own policies and legal requirements.

15. Deleting Detoxx Data

Users can manage or delete Detoxx data using the controls provided inside the App and through Android's application settings, subject to any active Detox session protection requirements.

During an active Detox session, certain session-critical information may be protected from ordinary clearing so that the active session and its timer remain consistent.

Removing the application or clearing application storage through Android may remove locally stored Detoxx information, subject to Android system behavior.

16. Children's Privacy

Detoxx is a digital wellness application. The App does not intentionally collect personal profile information from children.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted to Detoxx in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us at detoxxsupport@gmail.com.

17. Permissions

Detoxx requests only the Android permissions and special access capabilities necessary for supported features.

Depending on the features enabled, these may include Usage Access, Accessibility Service, notifications, VPN functionality, and other Android capabilities.

Users can review and revoke applicable permissions through Android system settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the associated Detoxx feature from functioning.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Detoxx's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change.

The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
